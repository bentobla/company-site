2. Scope of This Statement

This statement applies to the sudøku mobile app and related public app listing pages (Google Play and Apple App Store) where this statement is referenced.

3. Summary

sudøku is designed to work primarily on-device. Core gameplay data and settings are saved locally so the app can function without a user account.

  • No account is required to use the app
  • No user profile or login system is used
  • No custom backend server is used for gameplay synchronization
  • No analytics SDK and no crash reporting SDK are integrated in the current build
  • No third-party behavioral tracking is intentionally used by us
  • Third-party app store and ad services may process limited technical data when those features are active

4. Information Stored or Processed

4.1 Locally Stored on Your Device

The app may store the following data locally on your device for functionality:

  • Current puzzle state and gameplay progress
  • Notes, hints, timer values, and mistake counters
  • Statistics such as games played, wins, best times, and recent history
  • Settings, for example language, theme, and gameplay preferences
  • Premium entitlement or purchase restore state used by the app

4.2 Third-Party Processing (When Features Are Enabled)

Depending on platform and release configuration, third-party services may process data:

  • Google AdMob (ads): advertising identifier, IP address, device information, ad interaction, and diagnostics
  • Apple App Store / Google Play (purchases): transaction status and store-managed purchase metadata

Payment card details are processed by Apple or Google, not by us.

5. Purpose of Processing

Data described above is used to:

  • Run core gameplay and restore in-progress sessions
  • Maintain statistics and settings
  • Enable and verify premium entitlement
  • Display advertising in ad-supported builds
  • Meet legal and compliance requirements
  • Protect app integrity and security

6. Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

Where GDPR or similar law applies, legal bases may include:

  • Performance of contract: to provide requested app functionality
  • Legitimate interests: app stability, abuse prevention, and service operation
  • Consent: where required by law or platform rules (for example ad-related consent flows)
  • Legal obligation: compliance with applicable legal requirements

7. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be disclosed only in limited cases:

  • To service providers required for app operation (for example app stores and ad partners)
  • When required by law or valid legal request
  • In connection with merger, acquisition, or asset transfer

8. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on these providers depending on build and platform:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit
  • Google Play / Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Apple or Google in-app review prompt systems

Relevant external privacy policy links:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

9. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in your build, ads may be shown and ad network processing may occur according to the provider's policy and consent setup.

If advertising is not enabled in a given build or release channel, this section does not apply for that build.

10. In-App Purchases

  • Purchases are handled through Apple App Store or Google Play systems
  • The app stores only limited local entitlement state needed for restore/verification behavior
  • Billing details are not available to us

11. Data Retention

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

Third-party providers retain data under their own retention rules.

12. International Data Transfers

If third-party services are active, processing may occur in countries outside your own. Such transfers are governed by the relevant provider's legal safeguards and policies.

13. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures. No storage or transmission method is fully secure.

Because core app data is stored locally, device and backup security are also important and remain under your control.

14. Children's Privacy

The app is not intentionally directed to children below the age requiring parental consent under applicable law.

If you believe a child submitted data inappropriately, contact us and we will review the request.

15. Your Rights

Depending on your location, rights may include access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, data portability, and complaint rights with a supervisory authority.

For on-device app data, deletion is typically possible via app reset features or uninstalling the app.

16. In-App Deletion / Reset

sudøku provides local-data reset/deletion options in current builds.

  • Statistics reset and local progress reset are available in app settings
  • Uninstalling the app removes local app data from the device sandbox
