2. Scope

This statement applies to the sudøku mobile app and to public app-store pages that reference it.

3. Privacy at a Glance

  • No account, user profile, or login is required.
  • Puzzles, game progress, statistics, and settings are handled primarily on your device.
  • No custom backend is used to synchronize gameplay data.
  • The current app does not integrate an analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK.
  • The ad-supported version uses Google AdMob. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized, but Google may still process technical data required to provide, secure, measure, and comply with advertising services.
  • Apple or Google process purchase information when you make or restore an in-app purchase.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

For app functionality, sudøku may store locally:

  • current puzzle state, progress, notes, hints, timer values, and mistake counters;
  • statistics such as games played, wins, best times, and recent history;
  • language, appearance, and gameplay settings;
  • limited premium-entitlement or purchase-restore state.

This gameplay data is not uploaded to a Jetzt & Dahanna server because the app has no gameplay backend.

Apple App Store / Google Play

Purchases and restores are handled by Apple or Google. They process transaction and store-account information under their own policies. We do not receive your payment-card details.

In-app review

The app may invoke the platform's Apple or Google review-prompt system. Any processing by the store platform is governed by that provider.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by Apple, Google, and Google AdMob where the corresponding feature is used, or disclosed where required by law. Because core gameplay data remains local, it is not routinely shared with us.

8. Advertising

The free/ad-supported version may display ads through Google AdMob. Requests from sudøku are configured as non-personalized. “Non-personalized” does not mean that no data is processed: technical data may still be used by Google to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and meet legal requirements.

9. In-App Purchases

Where consent is legally required for a child to use advertising-related services, the applicable platform/provider rules and consent requirements apply.
