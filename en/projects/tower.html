tøwer

Your tower. Your airspace.

One more landing, then the next takeoff: in tøwer, you take control of air traffic. Give your aircraft clear instructions and guide them safely to their destination as you travel from airport to airport.

A look inside tøwer

A fresh challenge every shift

Take charge of takeoffs and landings

Plan flight paths, adjust heading and speed, and choose the right moment for the next clearance.

A journey around the world

Discover airports with different runway layouts. Each new destination gives you a fresh reason to think ahead.

Keep your cool as traffic builds

As traffic grows and conditions change, timing makes all the difference. Stay alert and work your way through increasingly demanding shifts.

How tøwer began

The idea for tøwer came from my son: a game of our own where you guide aircraft through takeoffs and landings. That idea grew into our little air traffic control game.
