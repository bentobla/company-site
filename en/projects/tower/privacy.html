Privacy Statement for tøwer

1. Controller and scope

The controller for tøwer is Tobias Blankenhorn, Jetzt & Dahanna Technologies, Richard-Wagner-Str. 17/1, 71116 Gärtringen, Germany. Email: info@jetztunddahanna.com.

This statement covers the current Android and iOS app and visits to this privacy page. Last updated: 24 October 2026.

2. Data in the app

tøwer requires no account or login. Gameplay works offline. Saves, active shifts, results, scores, stars, medals, unlocked content and settings are stored on your device. An optional display name is also stored locally; you do not need to provide your real name.

There is no developer-operated gameplay server, online leaderboard, analytics or crash-reporting integration, or automatic upload of gameplay or diagnostic data. The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. It does not ask for an age or date of birth.

3. Advertising and purchases

Native advertising is disabled in the current version. The app therefore does not initialize AdMob or send advertising requests. A prepared advertising integration is not currently enabled.

In-app purchases and purchase restoration are not currently connected to a billing provider either. The app does not collect payment details. Installation, downloads, any purchase through the store and operating-system diagnostics you choose to share are separately processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

If advertising or in-app purchases are enabled in a future release, this statement will be updated before that release.

4. Optional diagnostic export and contact

The export function lets you create a local diagnostic file. It contains the app version and build identifier, mission identifier, rules version, simulation tick, result status, result reason and score. It excludes your display name and full save. The file is not sent automatically.

If you email us or send a file yourself, we process your email address, message and voluntary attachments to handle your request. Recipients are us and the email services used for delivery. We do not sell personal data or share support data for advertising. Disclosures required by law remain possible.

5. Purposes and legal bases

Where personal data is processed, local processing provides the gameplay features you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We handle related service requests on the same basis; other enquiries are handled in our legitimate interests in answering messages and resolving problems (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Legally required retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

A display name and contacting us are optional. Without contact details, we may be unable to answer a support request. We do not carry out automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects, or related profiling.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until changed or removed. “Reset progress” clears gameplay progress and results, but does not remove all settings, your display name or an already exported diagnostic file. For complete removal, clear the app's data on Android or delete the app together with its data on iOS. Offloading the app alone is not full deletion.

The app also keeps a local backup of the previous save. Device transfers and operating-system backups may contain additional copies depending on the platform and your settings; manage those copies through the relevant platform. We cannot read or delete local data remotely.

We keep support messages and voluntary attachments only as long as needed to resolve the enquiry and necessary follow-up questions, then delete them unless legal retention obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims require further retention.

7. Visiting this page and external providers

This static privacy page uses no scripts, analytics, cookies, web storage or remotely embedded fonts. It is served through GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical connection data, particularly IP addresses, for delivery and security. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in securely providing this required information (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Hosting-log retention is governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

GitHub, Apple and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. Their linked privacy information explains recipients, retention and applicable transfer safeguards, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. Offline gameplay itself does not transfer saves abroad.

8. Children and your rights

These statements also apply to children. The current app uses no personalized advertising, advertising tracking or online accounts. Parents can manage local data on the device and contact us with privacy questions.

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw any consent you have given with effect for the future. You may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Germany. Send requests to the email address above. For store or operating-system data processed independently of us, also contact Apple or Google.
