Privacy Statement for tøwer

1. Controller and scope

The controller for tøwer is Tobias Blankenhorn, Jetzt & Dahanna Technologies, Richard-Wagner-Str. 17/1, 71116 Gärtringen, Germany. Email: info@jetztunddahanna.com.

This statement covers the current Android and iOS app and visits to this privacy page. Last updated: 24 October 2026.

2. Data in the app

tøwer requires no account or login. Gameplay works offline. Saves, active shifts, results, scores, stars, medals, unlocked content and settings are stored on your device. An optional display name is also stored locally; you do not need to provide your real name.

There is no developer-operated gameplay server, online leaderboard, developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting integration outside the advertising services described below, or automatic upload of gameplay or diagnostic data. The app does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. It does not ask for an age or date of birth.

3. Advertising and one-time ad removal purchase

The free version of tøwer displays advertising through Google AdMob. Ad requests are configured as non-personalized. Google may process IP addresses, device and app information, device identifiers depending on the platform and permitted settings, ad interactions and technical diagnostics. This processing supports ad delivery and measurement, frequency capping, security and fraud prevention. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no personal data is processed. Google's processing and retention are governed by the linked Google privacy information.

An optional one-time purchase permanently removes advertising. No subscription is required. Purchases and restoration are handled through Apple App Store or Google Play. The relevant store processes account, payment and transaction data. We do not receive credit-card or bank details. The app stores the purchase status needed to recognize and restore ad-free access, including the product and transaction identifiers, store and validity status. Raw purchase receipts and payment tokens are not stored in the save.

Once ad-free access is activated, the app makes no further ad requests. Purchasing ad removal does not retroactively delete data already processed by providers. Apple or Google separately process installation, download and operating-system diagnostic data you choose to share under their own privacy policies.

4. Optional diagnostic export and contact

The export function lets you create a local diagnostic file. It contains the app version and build identifier, mission identifier, rules version, simulation tick, result status, result reason and score. It excludes your display name and full save. The file is not sent automatically.

If you email us or send a file yourself, we process your email address, message and voluntary attachments to handle your request. Recipients are us and the email services used for delivery. We do not sell personal data or share support data for advertising. Disclosures required by law remain possible.

5. Purposes and legal bases

Where personal data is processed, local processing provides the gameplay features you request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We handle related service requests on the same basis; other enquiries are handled in our legitimate interests in answering messages and resolving problems (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Legally required retention is based on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

A display name and contacting us are optional. Without contact details, we may be unable to answer a support request. Processing purchases and restoring ad-free access is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Advertising-related processing and accessing or storing device identifiers are based on consent where legally required (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) of Germany's TDDDG); otherwise, necessary security and fraud-prevention processing is based on our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not carry out automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects, or related profiling.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until changed or removed. “Reset progress” clears gameplay progress and results, but does not remove all settings, your display name, stored purchase status or an already exported diagnostic file. For complete removal, clear the app's data on Android or delete the app together with its data on iOS. Offloading the app alone is not full deletion.

The app also keeps a local backup of the previous save. Device transfers and operating-system backups may contain additional copies depending on the platform and your settings; manage those copies through the relevant platform. We cannot read or delete local data remotely. Local purchase status is retained while needed for ad-free access or until app data is removed. Store transactions remain subject to Apple's or Google's separate retention rules.

We keep support messages and voluntary attachments only as long as needed to resolve the enquiry and necessary follow-up questions, then delete them unless legal retention obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims require further retention.

7. Visiting this page and external providers

This static privacy page uses no scripts, analytics, cookies, web storage or remotely embedded fonts. It is served through GitHub Pages. GitHub processes technical connection data, particularly IP addresses, for delivery and security. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in securely providing this required information (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Hosting-log retention is governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

GitHub, Apple and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area. Their linked privacy information explains recipients, retention and applicable transfer safeguards, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses. Offline gameplay itself does not transfer saves abroad.

8. Children

These statements also apply to children. The app uses no personalized advertising or online accounts. Advertising services are subject to special safeguards for children; technical data may still be processed as described above. Parents can manage local data on the device and contact us with privacy questions.

9. Data deletion and user rights

We can delete only data we hold, such as your support emails, subject to applicable retention requirements. We have no direct access to AdMob data stored by Google and cannot delete it ourselves. Direct deletion requests for that data to Google using the linked privacy contact options. Our statutory data protection duties remain unaffected.

tøwer operates no user accounts and stores no personal gameplay or usage data on its own servers. Local saves, settings and the optional display name remain on your device. This is separate from information you voluntarily send us in a support request and data processed by external providers such as Google AdMob, Apple or Google.

Remove local app data

See section 6 for app-data removal, reset limitations and backups. Removing local data does not delete records held by Google or Apple.

Manage your advertising ID and Google data

Advertising-related data may be processed by Google AdMob as described in section 3. On supported Android devices, you can reset or delete your advertising ID under Settings > Privacy > Ads. Labels and availability may vary by Android version and manufacturer. Google's official advertising ID guidance explains the steps. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID does not automatically erase data already held by providers.

Google provides its own tools to manage and delete personal data:

  • Google – My Activity
  • Google – Privacy Center
  • Google – Privacy rights, settings and contact options

My Activity does not cover all data processed through AdMob and therefore cannot delete all AdMob data. The data you can manage or delete and applicable retention periods depend on the relevant Google service and legal requirements. For data processed independently by Google, use the privacy request contact options described by Google.

Your rights in relation to us

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw any consent you have given with effect for the future. You may complain to a data protection supervisory authority, particularly the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Germany.

You may also exercise your privacy rights in relation to us as the app provider, even though we do not operate our own user database. Email info@jetztunddahanna.com; no user account or additional deletion form is required. We assess requests concerning processing within our responsibility, such as support messages. We cannot remotely erase data held only on your device or carry out or guarantee complete deletion of data processed independently by Google or Apple. Provider tools do not replace your rights in relation to us.
