You can delete your account here even if the app is no longer installed. Sign in with the same Google or Apple account you used in the app.

What will be deleted?

Online games, conversations, assessments, server-side learning sets and login data are deleted automatically. Your credit balance is surrendered. Minimal records preventing duplicate purchase and starter-credit redemption are retained for a limited period.

Data stored on other devices and files exported to lœrn or shared elsewhere are not removed. Delete local account data in the app on each device; no login is required.

Public accounts are not enabled yet. This automatic deletion service will be activated before launch. No deletion request has been sent.

Google or Apple sign-in services load only when you select that provider. This website does not persist your login.
