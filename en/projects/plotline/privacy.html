Privacy at pløtline

Draft for public launch. Updated 10 October 2026. Public accounts are not enabled. These details will be reconciled with the deployed infrastructure before activation.

1. Controller and contact

Tobias Blankenhorn, Jetzt & Dahanna Technologies, Richard-Wagner-Str. 17/1, 71116 Gärtringen, Germany. Privacy enquiries: info@jetztunddahanna.com.

2. Data we process

We verify a Google or Apple identity token and derive an account identifier. Your email is not the account key and your real name is not copied into the game profile. The optional game profile contains your chosen name and form of address; a fictional name is welcome.

Online games store the selected scene, configuration, game profile, conversations, assessments and any generated learning sets. Credits require balances, bookings and verified purchase/refund records. Device checks process technical app/device proofs and challenge identifiers. Cloudflare also processes connection metadata, including IP addresses.

Progress, statistics, saved learning sets and local conversations stay on the device and do not currently sync between devices. Short trainer memories may be sent to the backend for continuity within a campaign. Apple login also stores an encrypted refresh token so we can revoke the connection when you delete your account.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Requested games, login, credits, purchases and learning sets are provided to perform the contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Security, abuse prevention and duplicate purchase prevention serve our legitimate interests in a secure and sustainable service (Article 6(1)(f)). Legally required accounting records are processed to meet legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). The final allocation and legitimate-interest assessment will be reviewed before launch.

Do not enter real patient information or other sensitive information about yourself or others. Cases are fictional; the service is designed for learning games, not processing real health cases.

4. Providers and transfers

Cloudflare runs the backend and storage. In AI mode, conversation content, game profile, scenario context and relevant trainer memories are sent to the OpenAI API. Assessments and learning-set generation also use the necessary conversation content. API data is not used for model training by default. store: false does not prevent every form of storage: OpenAI may retain content in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default, with longer retention in exceptional cases.

Google and Apple process login, device verification and purchases under their own privacy notices. Reading aloud uses system speech services; network voices may send text to their provider. You choose the recipient when sharing or exporting files.

Processor agreements, data locations and any safeguards required for transfers outside the EEA will be checked and specified here before public activation. We do not currently promise EU-only storage or processing.

5. Retention and deletion

Unfinished paid games remain available until you delete the game or account. Completed online games are deleted automatically 90 days after their confirmed completion; you can delete them earlier in the app. Local statistics and learning sets remain until you delete them.

Account deletion immediately blocks access. Online content deletion and revocation of the Apple connection continue automatically, including retries after temporary errors. A secret receipt allows status checks for up to seven days; creating a new account invalidates the old receipt.

Minimal pseudonymous starter/purchase redemption records in the operational service are retained for up to three years. Minimal balance/refund records remaining after account deletion are removed after three years. They contain no conversations or game profiles. Legally required accounting documents are separate and follow the applicable statutory retention periods; their precise classification will be finalized before launch.

Deletion in the app also removes that account’s local data and learning sets from that device. Web deletion cannot directly remove data on other devices. Files exported, shared or imported into lœrn and backups outside our operational service cannot be recalled. Provider logs and platform backups follow their retention cycles; we do not promise immediate physical erasure of every copy.

6. Your rights and self-service

In the app you can change your game profile, delete conversations/local data, export your data as JSON and delete your account. Web deletion works without an installed app. Deletion requires fresh authentication; the normal process does not require contacting support.

Subject to GDPR conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us for further requests. You may complain to a supervisory authority, including the Baden-Württemberg data protection authority.

7. Website and security

The website privacy notice also applies to website visits. The deletion page keeps login tokens in browser memory only; closing or reloading requires a new login. You can save a deletion receipt as a file. Provider login services load only after selection. Public app accounts use separate credentials and storage from private testing.

Local data uses the operating system’s protected app storage and is not end-to-end encrypted against the backend. Login secrets use secure OS storage. Actual logging, backup and data-location configuration still requires verification before activation.
