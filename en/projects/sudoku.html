Clean. Local. Made for solving.

sudøku

A focused Sudoku app built around the pleasure of solving. Fresh puzzles are generated directly on your device, with thoughtful tools when you want them and a clean board when you do not.

On-devicePuzzles are generated locally.
OfflineThe core game is ready wherever you are.
FocusedThe interface keeps the puzzle at the centre.

Made for the puzzle

sudøku is designed to make everyday solving comfortable: choose a difficulty, settle into the board, and use as much or as little assistance as suits you.

Fresh puzzles, made on your device

Puzzles are generated and validated locally, giving you a self-contained supply of boards without relying on a puzzle catalogue in the cloud.

A board designed for concentration

Clear controls, readable numbers, thoughtful feedback, and carefully chosen interactions keep attention on the solve.

Four difficulty levels

Choose the level that fits the moment, from a relaxed solve to a more demanding puzzle.

Useful, local statistics

Follow your solving history and performance while keeping the experience personal and on your device.

Thoughtful solving tools

Notes, hints, pause protection, error review, and a short correction window support real play without crowding the board.

Your look, your pace

Light and dark appearance, multiple color themes, and optional assistance let the app adapt to how you like to solve.

Offline by design

The core Sudoku experience runs on your device. Puzzle generation, solving logic, active games, and statistics do not depend on a puzzle backend or a user account. That keeps the game fast, resilient, and pleasantly self-contained.

The free version may use platform services for advertising and purchases; those services are separate from the on-device puzzle engine. See the privacy statement for details.
