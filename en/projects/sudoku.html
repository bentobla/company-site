Sudoku, without the noise

sudøku

A clean, focused Sudoku app built around the puzzle itself. Your puzzles are generated directly on your device, so there is no puzzle feed to load and no account you need to create.

Why sudøku feels different

A Sudoku app does not need a social feed, an account, or a server deciding which puzzle comes next. sudøku keeps the experience deliberately small: open it, choose a difficulty, and solve.

Fresh puzzles, made on your device

Puzzles are generated and validated locally. The app does not need to fetch a catalogue of boards from a server.

Designed to stay out of your way

Clear controls, readable boards, thoughtful feedback, and a visual system designed around concentration rather than engagement tricks.

Four difficulty levels

Choose the level that fits the moment, from a relaxed solve to a more demanding puzzle.

Useful, local statistics

See how you are doing without turning a quiet logic puzzle into another online profile.

Made for real play

Notes, hints, pause protection, error review, and a short correction window make everyday solving comfortable without crowding the board.

Your look, your pace

Light and dark appearance, multiple color themes, and optional assistance let the app adapt without changing the puzzle underneath.

Offline is a feature, not a fallback

The core Sudoku experience runs on your device. Puzzle generation, solving logic, active games, and statistics do not depend on a puzzle backend or a user account. That makes sudøku fast, resilient, and pleasantly self-contained.

The free version may use platform services for advertising and purchases; those services are separate from the on-device puzzle engine. See the privacy statement for details.

Just a good Sudoku

The goal is simple: a polished Sudoku app that respects the puzzle, your attention, and the fact that sometimes the nicest technology is the technology you barely notice.
